# Privacy policy

Overview
This website has no user accounts, no contact form, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not set any cookies ourselves.

Hosting
The website is delivered via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When the site is accessed, the provider processes technical data such as the IP address, date and time, the requested file and browser information. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the website securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Third-party services
When the page is accessed, content is loaded from Google (fonts, YouTube thumbnails and videos), Bandcamp (music player) and Spreadshirt (merch images). In doing so, your browser and device transmit data to these providers for technical reasons, mainly the IP address. The featured video on the home page uses YouTube's enhanced privacy mode. On the “Videos” page, the player is only loaded after a click. Further information can be found in the privacy policies of [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Bandcamp](https://bandcamp.com/privacy) and [Spreadshirt](https://www.spreadshirt.de/datenschutz).

External links
We link to Bandcamp, Spotify, Apple Music, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, our merch shop and ticket providers. The privacy policy of the respective provider applies only once you click on a link.

Local storage
The page stores your color scheme in the browser (localStorage) so that it looks right immediately on your next visit. The entry stays on your device, contains no personal data and can be deleted in your browser settings.
