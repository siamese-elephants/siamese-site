Third-party services
When you open the page, content is loaded from Google (fonts, YouTube thumbnails and videos), Bandcamp (music player) and Spreadshirt (merch images). Your browser and device technically transmit data, mainly your IP address, to these providers. The featured video on the home page uses YouTube's enhanced privacy mode. On the "Videos" page the player only loads after a click. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in an appealing presentation of the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More information in the privacy policies of [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Bandcamp](https://bandcamp.com/privacy) and Spreadshirt.

External links
We link to Bandcamp, Spotify, Apple Music, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, our merch shop and ticket providers. The privacy policy of the respective provider applies only once you click a link.

Local storage
The page stores your color scheme in the browser (localStorage) so it looks right immediately on your next visit. The entry stays on your device, contains no personal data and can be deleted in your browser settings.

Contact by email
If you write to us, we process your details to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We do not pass them on and delete them when they are no longer needed.
